# Capstone Fantasy

A student-themed, turn-based Java RPG for Object-Oriented Programming at the University of San Jose–Recoletos. The project is in its foundation stage.

## Build and run

Install JDK 17 or newer. From the project folder, run:

```sh
sh build.sh
java -jar build/capstone-fantasy.jar
```

The game opens at 960 × 720 and can be resized, maximized, or minimized. The battlefield keeps a 16:9 aspect ratio while the battle controls and monster list are overlaid on the scene; control panel backgrounds use 80% opacity. Text and related battle controls scale with the 16:9 viewport. Other screens also retain 16:9 proportions, with black bars when needed. Use **Up/Down** and **Enter** or **Space** on the main menu. On character selection, use **Left/Right** and **Enter** or **Space** to choose or remove students; choose the configured number of students, then press **Down** and **Enter** to continue. **Escape** goes back. On the campus route, use **Left/Right** to move between unlocked locations and **Enter** to start the current encounter. All five route encounters are playable; the final encounter is the Crystal Guardian. After victory, press Enter to visit camp. At camp, use **Up/Down** to choose a student, **Enter** to use a First-Aid Kit, **I** to open Inventory & Equipment, **S** to save, and **C** to return to the route. In the inventory screen, **Up/Down** selects a student and **Left/Right** selects a weapon, armor, or accessory slot. Press **Enter** to choose a compatible item from the shared bag or unequip; the stat preview shows the effect before confirmation. Use **Page Up/Page Down** to scroll the shared bag. Press **Esc** to cancel or return to camp. Each item can be equipped by only one student, and weapons are class-specific. The party starts with its matching weapons, First-Aid Kits, a Campus Vest (+4 defense), and a Campus Pass key item. The first stage awards a Focus Charm (+2 attack) and a +3 attack weapon upgrade for each selected student; the second awards a Training Uniform (+6 defense). Stage rewards are granted once after victory. To replay a cleared stage, select it on the route and confirm **Yes**; use arrow keys to change the answer and Escape to cancel. Replays do not move route progress backward or repeat guaranteed stage rewards. Defeated Library Wisps award 12 Petot each, Stone Sentinels award 35, and the Crystal Guardian awards 150 on victory, including replays; the victory popup shows the amount credited. Each defeated Library Wisp has a 30% chance to drop a First-Aid Kit if the bag is below its nine-kit limit. Each Stone Sentinel has a 25% chance to drop the unique Sentinel Band (+2 defense) if the party does not already own it. Drops are awarded on victory and appear in the reward popup. From the route, **C** opens camp after the first victory. Carbon Market unlocks above the third route marker after clearing the third encounter. Select marker 3, press **Up** to select the shop, and **Enter** to visit; **Down** returns to the marker. In the market, use **Up/Down** and **Enter** to buy, or **Esc** to return to the route. The First-Aid Kit costs 20 Petot and the unique Market Charm (+1 attack, +1 defense) costs 60 Petot.

In battle use **Up/Down** and **Enter** or **Space** to choose Attack, Ability, Item, or Defend. Each living participant's bar fills smoothly at a default rate of 3 AP per second, up to their threshold: Zack 35, Bogart 30, Charice 28, Angel 50, Library Wisps A–F 24, 26, 28, 30, 32, and 34, and Stone Sentinel 32. Stage 1 generates 1–3 monsters, stage 2 generates 2–4, and stage 3 generates 3–5; stages 2 and 3 include one Stone Sentinel. Stage 4 uses 4–6 monsters; the final stage uses six including the boss, which occupies monster formation position 2. When a student's bar fills, that student is selected automatically. When no student is selected, a ready monster attacks automatically. Each participant's bar resets after its action. The clock pauses while a student is choosing a command or target; if multiple students are ready, they act in speed order before the clock resumes. A cursor shows the target on the battlefield: use arrow keys to move among living allies or enemies, Enter or Space to confirm, and Escape to cancel without using a turn or item. Red damage and green healing numbers rise and fade; attackers move briefly toward their target. Zack's Arcane Burst targets all living enemies. Use **Page Up/Page Down** to scroll longer battle messages. Angel's basic attack hits the selected target first, then selects random living enemies for up to two follow-up hits; if only one enemy was alive when the attack began, it hits at most twice. Angel can also use Healing Kiss. Students begin with SP: Zack 150, Bogart 50, Charice 45, and Angel 30. Their abilities cost 15, 5, 6, and 10 SP respectively. An ability requires sufficient SP; a failed attempt does not spend AP. SP is restored after a victory when the party returns to camp. Each student earns experience on victory, including knocked-out students; knocked-out students remain knocked out. The first level requires 100 XP; subsequent requirements grow according to the XP growth setting, up to the configured level cap. Each level adds 5 maximum HP, 2 ATK, 1 DEF and 3 maximum SP; living students also receive the additional HP and SP. Levels and XP are shown in battle rewards and saved. A graphical desktop is required.

Monsters use a skill on every third action. A Library Wisp casts Arcane Spark, a stronger single-target attack; a Stone Sentinel uses Stone Sweep, a weaker attack against each living student. Defend reduces damage from either skill, and battle messages and damage indicators show the results.

At camp, choose a student with the arrow keys and press **L** to allocate level-up points. Each level after the first grants one point in addition to the automatic level bonuses. Choose HP (+5 maximum), SP (+3 maximum), ATK (+1), DEF (+1), or SPD (+1) with the arrow keys and press Enter to spend a point; Esc returns to camp. HP increases also heal living students by 5, while SP increases restore 3 SP. SPD determines priority when multiple students are ready; it does not change their AP thresholds. Existing saves grant the unspent points earned at the saved level.

Normal battles start with the party AP bars full. At the start of each non-boss stage battle, there is a 50% chance of an ambush: party AP starts empty, monsters start with 80% of their AP, and enemies may act first. The final boss is exempt from ambushes. The stage retains its usual enemies and advances normally after victory. Entering camp does not trigger ambushes. The Settings menu selects Slow (2), Normal (3), or Fast (5) AP per second, applied equally to students and monsters. Animation frame rate remains independent.

## Game settings

Choose **Settings** on the title screen. Use Up/Down to choose a setting, Left/Right to adjust it, and Enter or Escape to save and return. Battle speed controls AP charge rate; XP growth ranges from 1% to 20% (default 7%), with the XP required to advance from level `n` calculated as `round(100 × (1 + growth / 100)^(n - 1))`. The level cap can be 20, 30, 40, or 50, and the party size can be one through four. Preferences are stored separately from saves in `~/.capstone-fantasy/settings.properties`.

Settings apply when starting or loading a game. Changing the XP curve preserves earned levels and relative progress. Lowering the cap below a character's level preserves that level but prevents further advancement until the cap is raised. If a loaded party has a different size than the current setting, choose the active students; inactive recruits keep their stats and equipment in reserve. Newly recruited students start at level 1.

## Optional classic fonts

Headings use Eight Bit Dragon by Chequered Ink and body labels use Gome Pixel by Fitrah Type when installed locally. Neither font is included in this repository or the game archive. On macOS, install the original `EightBitDragon-anqx.ttf` and `GomePixel-Regular.otf` in `~/Library/Fonts/`. To use files elsewhere, launch with `-Dcapstone.font=/path/to/EightBitDragon-anqx.ttf` and `-Dcapstone.bodyFont=/path/to/GomePixel-Regular.otf` before `-jar`. Missing fonts fall back to a built-in font. The Gome Pixel demo replaces digits with placeholders, so numeric game information uses a legible built-in font.

[Eight Bit Dragon](https://www.fontspace.com/eight-bit-dragon-font-f30428) has a non-commercial license restricting redistribution. [Gome Pixel](https://www.fontspace.com/gome-pixel-font-f119163) is a personal-use demo. Review their licenses before distributing the game or using it commercially.

## Saving and loading

At camp, press **S** to open the save screen. Choose one of three slots with **Up/Down** and select text or binary with **Left/Right**. Press Enter to save; replacing an existing save requires a second Enter, and Esc cancels. Text and binary saves are independent files, so saving one does not update the other. The main menu's **Load Game** opens the same slot and format selector and returns a loaded game to camp. Saves are kept in `~/.capstone-fantasy/saves/` and contain the current and reserve party, level-up allocations, progression rules, levels, experience, HP, SP, Petot balance, shared bag, equipment slots, claimed stage rewards, key item, and unlocked route position. Older saves remain supported: saves from before Petot start at zero; saves from before SP start with full SP, their base stats update to the current values, and their vest ownership and kits are preserved, starter weapons are assigned, and rewards for already completed stages are added once. Missing or invalid files show a message. This version saves at camp after any of the five battles.

To run the save regression check after building:

```sh
mkdir -p build/test-classes
javac -cp build/capstone-fantasy.jar -d build/test-classes tests/*.java
java -cp build/capstone-fantasy.jar:build/test-classes EquipmentSystemTest
java -cp build/capstone-fantasy.jar:build/test-classes SaveStoreTest
java -Djava.awt.headless=true -cp build/capstone-fantasy.jar:build/test-classes RouteBattleTest
java -Djava.awt.headless=true -cp build/capstone-fantasy.jar:build/test-classes AnimationTest
```

## Direction

The game is intended to be suitable for a school audience, without profanity, innuendo, sexual content, or immoral themes. Its battle layout takes inspiration from classic JRPGs and [Sara's Turn Based Battle System](https://sarajs.itch.io/saras-turn-based-battle-system); the application is being built in Java Swing. The game uses a finer, 32-bit-inspired pixel-art treatment. The battlefield and student portraits are rendered at low resolution and scaled with nearest-neighbor interpolation. The in-game shell also uses a reduced-resolution rendering layer for a consistent pixel-art appearance; native window controls remain unchanged.

## Known limitations

Five encounters and a camp are implemented. HP, knockouts, and remaining First-Aid Kits carry between battles; kits cannot revive knocked-out students. Saving currently captures only the available camp state. The inventory currently has one consumable and one key item; additional consumables and later-stage rewards depend on future encounters. The requested independent AP system and stage ambush allow enemies to act before the party, so combat does **not** satisfy the course brief's party-first turn rule; instructor approval or a compliant mode is needed. Stats and balance are provisional, sprite movement uses simple position animation rather than multiple animation frames, and the route markers and paths are drawn by the application over the campus artwork.
